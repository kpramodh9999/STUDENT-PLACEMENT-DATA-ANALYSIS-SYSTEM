About Placement Cell Portal

The Campus Training & Placement Cell Portal provides statistical transparency and visibility into institutional career outcomes, recruiter partnerships, department placement records, and engineering specialization metrics across graduation batches 2018 through 2026.

Entities Analyzed:

  • Students, technical competencies, and eligibility tracking
  • Campus placement offers, recruiters, and salary packages (in LPA)
  • Department and sub-branch comparative statistics
  • Compensation brackets and salary percentiles calculated via NumPy

Help & Placement Support

1. How is the Placement Rate calculated?

(Unique Eligible Placed Students ÷ Total Eligible Students) × 100. Students with multiple job offers are deduplicated so placement rate is accurate.

2. Sub-Branch Filtering

Selecting Computer Science or Electronics & Communication automatically activates the sub-branch filter for specialized tracking.

3. Training & Placement Office Contact

Email: placement@college.edu • Office: Block A, Room 204
